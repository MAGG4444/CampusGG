# Tutorial CampusGG

## Overview

CampusGG is a web platform that helps college students find gaming teammates, join lobbies, and discuss games with other players.

This tutorial covers account registration, gaming lobbies, forum discussions, and user profiles.

## Prerequisites

- A device with internet access and a web browser.
- A university email address ending in `.edu`.
- Access to your university email inbox.

## Setup / Installation

CampusGG requires no installation.

1. Open your web browser.
2. Visit [https://campusgg.net](https://campusgg.net).

## First Workflow

### Step 1: Create an Account

1. Click **Sign In** in the navigation bar, then **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your university email, username, display name, and password.
3. Confirm your password and click **Sign Up**.

You will be redirected to the email verification page.

### Step 2: Verify Your Email

1. Get the six-digit verification code from your university email inbox.
2. Enter the code on the **Verify Your Email** page.
3. Click **Verify Email**.

If you do not receive the code, click **Resend code**.

After verification, you will be redirected to the Sign In page.

### Step 3: Sign In

1. Enter your registered email and password.
2. Click **Sign In**.

You will return to the homepage, and **Profile** will replace **Sign In** in the navigation bar.

### Step 4: Find a Gaming Lobby

1. Click **Lobby** in the navigation bar.
2. Browse the available lobbies.
3. Use the game and skill level filters or search bar to find a suitable lobby.

### Step 5: Join a Lobby

1. Click **Join Lobby** on a lobby card.
2. View the lobby information and members on the Lobby Details page.

The lobby will be marked as **Joined**. You can return using **Open Lobby** or leave by clicking **Leave**.

### Step 6: Create a Lobby

1. Return to the **Lobby** page and click **+ Create Lobby**.
2. Enter a lobby name, description, and details.
3. Select a tag, participant limit, and **Public** visibility.
4. Click **Publish**.

Your new lobby will appear in the Lobby Feed. You can also use **Save** to keep a draft.

### Step 7: Explore the Forum Feed

1. Click **Forum** in the navigation bar.
2. Browse posts or use the search bar to find a topic.
3. Click **Read More** to view a post and its comments.

You can also add a comment to join the discussion.

### Step 8: Create a Forum Post

1. Return to **Forum** and click **+ Create Post**.
2. Enter a post title and content.
3. Optionally, add a short description, game name, and tag.
4. Keep **Public** visibility selected and click **Publish**.

You will be redirected to your published post. Use **Save** if you want to keep a draft instead.

### Step 9: View Your Profile

1. Click **Profile** in the navigation bar.
2. View your account information and gaming preferences.
3. Click **My Content** to access your posts, lobbies, and drafts.

Click **Sign Out** to log out.

## Expected Results

After completing this tutorial, you should be able to:

- Create an account, verify your email, and sign in.
- Find, join, and create gaming lobbies.
- Browse forum discussions and publish posts.
- Save unfinished content as drafts.
- View your profile and manage your content.

## Troubleshooting

### 1. I cannot create an account.

Make sure your email ends in `.edu`, all required fields are completed, and your passwords match.

### 2. I did not receive a verification code.

Check your spam folder or click **Resend code**.

### 3. I cannot sign in.

Check your email and password, and make sure your email is verified.

### 4. I cannot find or join a lobby.

Clear your filters, make sure you are signed in, and check whether the lobby is full or invitation-only.

### 5. I cannot publish a lobby or forum post.

Make sure you are signed in, complete all required fields, and check any error messages.

### 6. I cannot see forum posts.

Clear the search bar, select **All Categories**, or refresh the page.

### 7. I cannot access my Profile page.

Make sure you are signed in. If redirected to Sign In, log in again.
